2. **Verify auxiliary contracts** — confirm and document any auxiliary or related contracts where applicable.

5. **Implement real DRAC utility** — integrate DRAC into actual Dracul Cybersec products and services before speculative marketing.
6. **Evaluate additional pools** — consider adding liquidity to additional pools only when sufficient organic liquidity exists.
7. **Market-data listings** — consider listings on market-data aggregators at a later stage.
