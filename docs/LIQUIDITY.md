## Liquidity policy

Liquidity is currently experimental/small. The project does not endorse wash trading, fake volume, coordinated price manipulation or misleading market activity.

Because the pool is shallow:

- relatively small swaps can create large percentage price changes;
- slippage can be extreme;
- quoted FDV/market-cap figures can be misleading;
- additional pools could fragment liquidity.

The current strategy is to keep the WETH/DRAC v2 pool as the canonical market while liquidity remains small.
